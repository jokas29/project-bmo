# DropEC — Content Pack Launch 01

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Status: **DRAFT / DO NOT PUBLISH WITH PURCHASE CTA UNTIL SALES ARE SAFELY ACTIVATED**

This pack is written from clean product facts and launch economics. Do not paste supplier descriptions, supplier phone numbers, wholesale instructions, external Drive links, or unsupported claims into customer content.

## Global short-form rules

- 9:16 first.
- Target 12–22 seconds.
- Show the product/result in the first 2 seconds.
- Use large on-screen text with one idea per shot.
- Price can appear near the end, not as the opening frame.
- CTA: DM keyword; checkout remains conversational.
- Never claim universal free shipping; shipping is quoted by destination.
- Never claim a fake discount or fake scarcity.

# Trust content — publish around the launch

These posts reduce purchase anxiety without pretending DropEC already has reviews it does not have.

## Trust Post 1 — How ordering works

**Headline:** `Comprar por DM no tiene que ser complicado.`

**Body:**
`1. Nos escribes por el producto.\n2. Confirmamos tus datos.\n3. Cotizamos el envío según tu ciudad.\n4. Te enviamos el enlace de pago seguro.\n5. Confirmamos el pago automáticamente.\n6. Procesamos y te avisamos el avance del pedido.`

Do not publish step 4 as active until payment-link release is actually enabled.

## Trust Post 2 — Why shipping is not a fake flat rate

**Headline:** `¿Cuánto cuesta el envío?`

**Body:**
`Lo cotizamos según tu destino antes de que pagues. Así ves el total real de tu pedido y no te damos una cifra inventada para todo Ecuador.`

## Trust Post 3 — Payment safety

**Headline:** `Un link no significa que ya pagaste.`

**Body:**
`DropEC solo procesa el pedido cuando el proveedor de pagos confirma la transacción. No te pediremos reenviar capturas como sustituto de una confirmación válida.`

## Trust Post 4 — Tracking

**Headline:** `Después del pago, no desaparecemos.`

**Body:**
`Cuando tu pedido avanza por preparación y envío, DropEC conserva el estado para poder informarte durante el proceso.`

Avoid promising exact delivery times until carrier data supports them.

# Story template sequence for any launch product

1. **Problem:** one sentence + visual.
2. **Demo:** product solving the problem.
3. **Detail:** one feature visible on camera.
4. **Price:** current catalog price.
5. **CTA:** `Escríbenos “KEYWORD” por DM`.
6. **FAQ:** shipping is quoted by city.

---

# Objection-response content ideas

Turn real customer objections into future posts. Do not fabricate objections as testimonials.

- `¿Hacen envíos a mi ciudad?` → shipping explainer.
- `¿Cuánto queda con envío?` → explain quote-before-payment.
- `¿Cómo pago?` → PayPhone safe-payment explainer after activation.
- `¿Cómo sé si salió mi pedido?` → tracking lifecycle explainer.
- `¿Qué incluye?` → product-specific unboxing/component post.

---

# Creative rotation rule

For each active product, do not generate ten completely unrelated concepts. Test systematically:

- same demo, different 0–2s hook;
- same hook, different first visual;
- same creative, different CTA wording;
- then a genuinely different angle.

A winning demonstration should be iterated, not discarded.
